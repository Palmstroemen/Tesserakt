## The Apparatus Looks Back

The magic eye shows what lies behind your device, and your face is reflected in the glass of the result.
For this, the Apparatus uses your device's cameras – **only for these effects**.

The images stay on your device. We record nothing, store nothing and send nothing.

The game works just the same without a camera. The eye simply stays a little calmer.

## Playing for Science

Tesserakt is a game and an experiment at the same time: do horoscopes hit the mark more often than chance
allows?

If you agree, we store your answers and the results for scientific analysis – **without your name and
without your place of birth**. Exactly what is stored is described in the detailed explanation.

**One request:** If you agree, please answer honestly and thoughtfully. Only sincere answers help the research.
If you'd rather just try it out, play without consent – that's perfectly fine, and the game is the same.
