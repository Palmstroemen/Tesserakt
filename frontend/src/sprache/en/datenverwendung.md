# What Happens to Your Data

## In Short

- The camera images never leave your device.
- Without your consent, we store nothing of what you enter in the game.
- With your consent, we store your answers and results – without your name, without your place of birth,
  without your exact date of birth.
- We never sell your data and never use it for advertising.
- You can always play without consent. The game is the same.

## The Camera

The Apparatus uses two of your device's cameras for effects: the rear camera lets light shine through the magic
eye, the front camera reflects your face in the glass of the result. The images are shown only on your device. We
record nothing, store nothing and send nothing to us or to anyone else.

## What We Need for the Calculation

For the horoscopes we need your date of birth, the time and the place. The Apparatus sends this information to
our server for the calculation. The server calculates with it and then forgets it – whether you consent or not.

## What We Store If You Consent

- your answers to the questions and which game you played; if you go back a question, also your first answer
  and how often you changed it (the first answer always counts for the analysis)
- the results of the horoscopes (how well each one matched your answers)
- your year of birth
- whether you set the time and the place of birth (only yes or no)
- your cultural region, e.g. Europe, India or China
- the language of the game and the date of the game

## What We Never Store

- your name or anything else that directly identifies you
- your place of birth, your exact date of birth and your time of birth
- the calculated values of the horoscopes themselves (your date of birth could be worked out from them)
- in Game C, no birth data of your partner, not even the year
- camera images
- your email address, phone number or device ID

## Why Your Answers Are Especially Protected

Your answers are personal: you say something about how you handle money, for example, or about your health.
That is why we store them separately from everything else and only in a form that cannot be traced back to you.
We use answers about health only in aggregated form.

## What For

Solely for the question of whether horoscopes hit the mark more often than chance. We publish the results as a
scientific analysis and as an open dataset so that others can check them. You cannot be identified in it.

**We do not sell data and do not use it for advertising.** It would not even be suitable for that: we have no
names, no addresses, no email addresses and no phone numbers. Nobody can infer anything about you personally
from what we store – not even us.

## How Long

As long as the experiment runs and the analysis is meant to remain verifiable. Because the stored data does not
identify you, we cannot assign it to you later either – and therefore cannot delete it individually. You can
change your decision for future games at any time.

## Verifiable

The source code of the Apparatus and of our server is open: **github.com/Palmstroemen/Tesserakt**. Anyone can
read there what the Apparatus does with the camera, what it sends to the server and what the server stores.
The server is licensed under AGPL-3.0, the Apparatus under MIT.

Copies of the Apparatus with their own server collect their answers separately from ours. Each copy splits the
sample, and none of the small datasets is then large enough for an answer. The experiment only succeeds if as
many people as possible play on the same Apparatus.

## Your Rights

You have the right to access, rectification and erasure, insofar as we can assign your data to you, and the
right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority (in Austria: Datenschutzbehörde, dsb.gv.at).
