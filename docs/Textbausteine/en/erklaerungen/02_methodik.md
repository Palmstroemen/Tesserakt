# How the horoscopes calculate {#methodik}

Kurz: From your date of birth we calculate the classical horoscope of each tradition. Handed-down rules of interpretation translate it into tendencies for eight areas of life – and those are exactly what we compare with your answers.

## From date of birth to horoscope {#vom_datum_zum_horoskop}

Each horoscope looks at something different. Western astrology calculates where the Sun, Moon and planets stood at the moment of your birth. Bazi forms the four pillars of the Chinese calendar from the year, month, day and hour. Numerology works with the digits of your date of birth and – if you give it – with the letters of your name.

Time and place of birth matter mainly for the astrological horoscopes: they determine, for example, the Ascendant and the houses. If you do not know them exactly, these parts become less precise.

## The seven horoscopes {#die_systeme}

- **Western:** zodiac signs, planets in signs and houses, aspects between the planets and current transits.
- **Bazi (Chinese):** the four pillars, your Day Master, the interplay of the five elements and the current Luck Pillar.
- **Numerology:** Life Path number, name numbers and personal cycles.
- **Kabbalah:** the Sephiroth and paths of the Tree of Life that are assigned to your date of birth.
- **Arabic:** the Arabic lots, such as the Lot of Fortune, and the Firdaria – stretches of life ruled by changing planets.
- **Hellenistic:** the astrology of antiquity, with the birth sign, annual profections and the Lord of the Year, day or night birth (sect) and the lots.
- **Vedic (Jyotish):** your Moon Nakshatra, the planetary periods (Dasha) and special configurations (Yogas).

## Rules become numbers {#regeln_zu_zahlen}

We did not invent the interpretations. They come from the handed-down textbooks of each tradition – from Ptolemy and Vettius Valens through Abu Ma'shar to Parashara and more recent standard works.

Each rule says something like: "This placement favors relationships" or "This phase brings unrest in your career". We translated such statements into numbers for eight areas of life: love, career, finances, health, social life, creativity, change and spirituality. For each area this yields a tendency between "rather does not apply" and "rather applies".

## The comparison with you {#vergleich}

Your 16 answers are spread across the same eight areas of life. For each question we compare how close a horoscope's tendency comes to your own assessment. Some questions count more for one horoscope than for another – depending on how much the tradition has to say on that topic.

## Limits {#grenzen}

Every translation simplifies. The traditions are richer than eight numbers can capture, and astrologers would weigh some things differently. Our result therefore shows how well **our translation** of a horoscope tradition fits you – not the last word on the tradition itself.
